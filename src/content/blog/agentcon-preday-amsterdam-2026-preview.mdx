The day before AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe takes over Amsterdam, the city's AI and cloud-native communities are warming up with something more intimate. On **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**, the **AgentCon Preday Meetup — Agentic AI in the Wild: Securing What Runs in Production** brings together the people actually putting autonomous agents into real systems.

I'll be there, and if you build or operate AI agents in production, this is the room you want to be in the night before the main conference.

## Why this meetup matters

AI agents have crossed the demo boundary. They write code, open pull requests, touch dependencies, and run inside CI/CD pipelines. The interesting question is no longer "should we use agents?" — it's "can we trust what they ship?"

That's the thread this evening pulls on. The conversations are the ones that rarely fit a conference talk:

- **What breaks when an agent holds write access to your dependencies** — the supply-chain attack surface a junior engineer never had
- **Encoding your team's standards as machine-readable guardrails** — turning tribal knowledge into something an agent can be forced to obey
- **What changes when agentic systems run at cloud-native scale** — context, governance, and failure modes that only show up under load

No vendor pitches. Just practitioners comparing notes on systems that are live today.

## Speakers to watch

The lineup pairs platform-engineering voices with security practitioners:

- **Liran Tal** — Senior Director, Developer Relations, Snyk. His session sits at the intersection of developer experience and the security reality of agentic systems.
- **Nnenna Ndukwe** — Developer Relations, Qodo. Qodo's angle is continuous, AI-powered code review — quality gates that move at agent speed.
- **Panel: Agentic AI in the Wild** — moderated by **Dana Fine**, with **Brian Vermeer** (Java Community), **Carlos Mestre del Pin** (Microsoft), **Alessandro Stefouli-Vozza** (Cloud Native Community), and a speaker TBA from **OpenAI**. Expect concrete lessons on context management, governance, production workflows, and failure modes — not talking points.

If you're in Amsterdam for AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe the next two days, this Preday is the cheapest networking you'll do all week — a smaller room, a no-pitch rule, and the people who are furthest along in actually running agents in production. Show up early; the doors open at 18:00.
