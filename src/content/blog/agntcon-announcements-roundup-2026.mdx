# AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026: All the Announcements

The biggest agentic AI conference in Europe delivered a packed agenda of announcements. Here's the complete roundup from **AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026** at RAI Amsterdam.

## Core Infrastructure

### MCP Graduates to Enterprise Infrastructure

The **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** received its biggest update since launch — introducing a stateless core, new extensions, stronger enterprise authorization, and a formal deprecation policy. This addresses the scaling concerns that kept MCP out of enterprise environments.

[Read more on what this means](/blog/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-2026/#mcp-graduates-to-enterprise-infrastructure)

### Envoy AI Gateway Becomes Agent Router, Joins AAIF

**Envoy AI Gateway** is now **Agent Router** and joins the Agentic AI Foundation as a hosted project. Developers connect through OpenAI-compatible APIs and MCP endpoints, while platform teams manage providers, credentials, quotas, and routing.

### A2A Joins the Open Agentic Stack

**Agent2Agent (A2A)** — the open standard for inter-agent communication — joins MCP under the AAIF umbrella. While MCP handles agent-to-tool connectivity, A2A standardizes agent-to-agent communication.

*(Individual announcement blog posts for each of these are being published separately.)*

## Industry Adoption

### Agentic AI Foundation Welcomes 57 New Members

Including **Alibaba**, **Visa**, and **Wells Fargo** as new Gold Members, plus 33 Silver and 21 Associate Members across financial services, enterprise technology, research, and global organizations.

### Gates Foundation Joins AAIF

The **Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation** becomes the first philanthropy to join AAIF, bringing global perspective across health, education, agriculture, and economic opportunity.


### MCPA Certification Launched

The **Model Context Protocol Associate (MCPA)** certification validates engineering and platform builders' understanding of MCP concepts, architecture, and implementation.


## Product Launches

### Traefik Labs: Sovereign Trust Plane

Cryptographically verifiable evidence of what AI agents were authorized to do, under which policies, and whether records were altered. Built into Traefik Hub.


### WSO2: Agent Manager

Open control plane governing AI agents across any framework, model, or deployment — helping enterprises put agents in production with confidence.


### Grape Up: Aiboostr

Open-source AI Governance & Orchestration Platform with EU AI Act compliance, audit trails, and private LLM support — all under one control plane.


### AgentCat: AX Platform

The first **Agent Experience (AX)** platform — combining analytics to capture unmet demand from AI agents, goal detection, session replay, and task observability.


### Alpic AI: AI Assistants as Business Channel

With MCP in production, AI assistants are becoming an acquisition channel for brands like PGA of America, AirHelp, and Novasol, and a way to give enterprise customers AI-native product experiences.
