## People I Met at AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026

Conferences are as much about the people as the protocols. Over three days in Amsterdam — the AGNTCon PreDay meetup at Adyen and two full days of AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe at RAI Amsterdam — I met the people actually building the agentic stack: protocol maintainers, platform engineers, developer advocates, and community organizers. Here's who I connected with.

## Keynote Speakers

- **Angie Jones** — VP of Developer Experience, <img src="https://cdn.sanity.io/images/4o10fa7h/production/cc9de238d88dab9eebd13cb1e1cfd858ba39509e-32x32.png?w=64&h=64" alt="Agentic AI Foundation" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Agentic AI Foundation](https://aaif.io). Opened both Day 1 and Day 2, framing the conference around agentic AI as a production technology, not a future one.
- **David Soria Parra** — Member of Technical Staff, <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/anthropic" alt="Anthropic" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com), and co-creator of the Model Context Protocol. Announced MCP's move to a stateless core.
- **Dexter Horthy** — CEO & Co-founder, <img src="https://www.humanlayer.dev/favicon.ico" alt="HumanLayer" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[HumanLayer](https://www.humanlayer.dev). Coined "context engineering"; spoke on the state of the software factory.
- **Maarten Grootendorst** — Member of Technical Staff, <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/deepmind" alt="Google DeepMind" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google), creator of BERTopic, KeyBERT, and PolyFuzz.
- **Shaun Smith** — MCP and Transport Working Group Maintainer, <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/huggingface" alt="Hugging Face" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co). Deep dive into stateless MCP in production.
- **Clare Liguori** — Senior Principal Engineer, <img src="https://a0.awsstatic.com/libra-css/images/site/fav/favicon.ico" alt="AWS" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[AWS](https://aws.amazon.com), and core MCP specification maintainer.
- **Frédéric Barthelet** — CTO & Co-founder, <img src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/PdZs84lSywfOu3AgsUUvO9W0I.png" alt="Alpic" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Alpic](https://alpic.ai). Presented on MCP vs. WebMCP vs. CLI alongside Dominic Farolino.
- **Dominic Farolino** — Editor of the WebMCP specification, <img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/google" alt="Google" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Google](https://www.google.com).
- **Idit Levine** — Founder & CEO, <img src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66dba20a96c0aa281999f399/6a902345de227e28aa9a28f4_Favicon.png" alt="Solo.io" width="16" height="16" style="display:inline-block;vertical-align:-2px;margin-right:4px;border-radius:2px;" />[Solo.io](https://www.solo.io).

## Why This Matters

Every protocol and product announced at AGNTCon + MCPCon has a name — or several — behind it. What struck me across all three days was how small this community still is relative to its impact: the same handful of people building MCP, A2A, AGENTS.md, and the surrounding tooling are the ones you run into at the coffee line, the expo floor, and the PreDay networking session. If you're building on the agentic stack, these are the people worth following.
