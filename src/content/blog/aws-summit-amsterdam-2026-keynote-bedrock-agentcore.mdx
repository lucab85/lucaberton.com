I attended the **AWS Summit Amsterdam 2026** keynote on May 27, and the announcements were massive. From a new agentic AI platform to OpenAI models running on AWS infrastructure, here is everything that matters for enterprise builders.

## The Keynote: Agentic AI Takes Center Stage

The keynote, hosted by **Martin** from AWS, focused on a single theme: **AI agents are transforming what is possible**, and AWS wants to make it easier than ever to build, deploy, and scale them.

The session (KEY101) was structured around three pillars:
- **Infrastructure** — custom silicon and S3 as the universal data layer
- **Data** — open architecture with catalog and semantic layers
- **Inference** — taking AI from prototype to production

![AWS Summit Amsterdam 2026 S3 Platform](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-s3-platform.jpg)

## Amazon S3: The Universal Data Foundation

AWS positioned S3 as the starting point for everything — not just object storage, but a unified platform supporting:

- **Object** storage (classic S3)
- **Tabular** data (Iceberg, Spark)
- **Metadata** management
- **Vector** storage for AI/ML

The integrated ecosystem includes Analytics, Database, AI/ML, Search, and Streams — with Iceberg, Apache Spark, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch, and Kafka all running on top of S3.

### Amazon S3 Files (NEW)

A new service providing **fully-featured, high-performance file system access** to S3 data. You can mount any S3 bucket inside EC2, Lambda, ECS, EKS, or Fargate — eliminating the need for separate file storage solutions.

### Open Data Architecture

AWS introduced a **Catalog and Semantic Layer** sitting above the service tiers, providing metadata, business context, governance rules, and semantic meaning across all data assets.

## Amazon Bedrock: Broadest Model Choice

![Amazon Bedrock Model Catalog](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-bedrock-models.jpg)

Bedrock now offers the **broadest choice of models** from 18+ providers:

- **AI21Labs** (Jamba), **Amazon** (Nova), **Anthropic** (Claude Opus, Sonnet, Haiku)
- **Cohere** (Command, Embed, Rerank), **DeepSeek**, **Google** (Gemma)
- **Moonshot AI** (Kimi), **Luma** (Ray), **Meta** (Llama), **MiniMax**
- **Mistral** (Mistral, Mixtral, Pixtral), **NVIDIA** (Nemotron)
- **OpenAI** (GPT, GPT-O3S, GPT-O3S-Safeguard), **Qwen** (Qwen3-Next, Qwen3-VL)
- **Stability AI** (Stable Diffusion, Stable Image), **TwelveLabs** (Marengo, Pegasus), **Writer** (Palmyra)

The platform provides built-in security, customization, guardrails and policy checks, agentic AI capabilities, and models and infrastructure — all designed to take you from **prototype to production reliably, securely, and at scale**.

**"The power to choose. We don't force you into a single fixed path."**

## Amazon Bedrock AgentCore (NEW)

![Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-agentcore.jpg)

The biggest announcement: **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** — an agentic platform to build, deploy, and operate agents securely at scale.

### The Problem

"Getting agents to production is still difficult." The journey from Logic → Context → Secure → Deploy → Audit → Scale requires solving many undifferentiated challenges. AgentCore lets you **focus on innovation rather than operations**.

### Nine Components

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Runtime** | Agent execution environment |
| **Memory** | Persistent agent state |
| **Gateway** | API management and routing |
| **Identity** | Authentication and authorization |
| **Observability** | Native monitoring and tracing |
| **Code Interpreter** | Safe code execution |
| **Managed Browser** | Web interaction capabilities |
| **Evaluations** | Quality and safety testing |
| **Agent Registry** (NEW) | Catalog and discover agents |

## Amazon Bedrock Managed Agents — Powered by OpenAI (NEW, Preview)

![Bedrock Managed Agents powered by OpenAI](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-managed-agents.jpg)

**"Frontier intelligence meets enterprise-grade infrastructure."**

This is a landmark partnership: OpenAI models (including **GPT 5.5**) running inside Amazon Bedrock with full governance and observability.

**Runtime layer:**
- Agentic Loop, Inference, Memory, Skills (OpenAI Harness)

**Environment layer:**
- Compute, Storage, Networking, Security, Tools/MCP

**Foundation:**
- OpenAI Models (including GPT 5.5)
- Amazon Bedrock Governance and Observability

This means enterprises can now use the most capable OpenAI models while keeping AWS security, compliance, and infrastructure guarantees.

## Customer Story: KBC Bank — Kate, the AI Banking Agent

![Karen Van De Woestyne at AWS Summit](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kbc-karen.jpg)

**Karen Van De Woestyne**, General Manager Transform and Ecosphere at KBC Bank and Verzekering, presented perhaps the most impressive enterprise AI deployment at the summit.

### Three Shifts That Define KBC's Future

![KBC Three Shifts](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kbc-shifts.jpg)

Karen outlined three fundamental shifts driving KBC's transformation:

- **Transactions → Journeys** — moving from isolated interactions to supporting complete life paths
- **Asking → Anticipating** — from reactive responses to proactive suggestions
- **Bank → Digital Buddy** — from financial institution to trusted companion

*"This is not just a story about my daughter Amber, 27, dreaming of leaving the home."*

KBC serves **6.1 million customers** and is shifting from reactive banking to proactively supporting life journeys — not just transactions.

### Intent Thinking: The Engine Behind Kate

![KBC Intent Thinking](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kbc-intent.jpg)

*"Intent Thinking turns signals into trusted action, so Kate can anticipate the next meaningful step."*

KBC built what they call an **Intent Engine** — a system that interprets customer signals (browsing patterns, life events, transaction history) and converts them into proactive, personalised recommendations. This is what powers Kate's ability to suggest the right financial product at the right moment.

### The Roadmap: From Now to the New Bliss

![KBC Roadmap 2025-2027](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kbc-roadmap.jpg)

- **2025**: BUILDING — establishing the AI foundation
- **2026**: ACCELERATE — scaling across customer journeys
- **2027**: AMBITION — full anticipatory banking

### Kate: 80 Million Conversations

![KBC Kate Statistics](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kate-stats.jpg)

**Kate** is KBC's trusted digital buddy, built on AWS:

- **80M conversations** started by customers
- **25K+ customer queries** answered every day
- **350+ FTE equivalent** operational output
- **50+ third-party services** integrated
- **400K+ products and services** sold per year

**Built on:** Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Amazon EKS, Amazon S3

### The Ecosphere: Beyond Banking

![KBC Ecosphere](/blog/events/aws-summit-amsterdam-2026-kbc-ecosphere.jpg)

Kate operates inside what KBC calls **The Ecosphere** — a customer universe extending beyond traditional banking:

- **Myhome**: 40K customers onboarded, 138K questions answered
- **Mymobility**: 340K customers onboarded, 1.5M+ train tickets issued, 97 cars sold

*"If we miss Amber's intent, someone else will own the moment, shape the relationship, and capture the value."*

*"It looks like when you have the intelligence and scale to back it up."*

## Key Takeaways

1. **S3 is no longer just storage** — it is the universal data platform spanning objects, tables, metadata, and vectors
2. **AgentCore solves the production gap** — the hardest part of agents is not building them, it is operating them at scale
3. **OpenAI on Bedrock is real** — GPT 5.5 with enterprise guardrails changes the competitive landscape
4. **Agents deliver measurable ROI** — KBC's 350 FTE equivalent and Signify's 27% cost reduction prove the business case
5. **The power to choose** — AWS is betting on model diversity, not lock-in

## My Take

The theme of this keynote was clear: **agentic AI is the next platform shift**, and the companies winning are those who have already moved past experimentation into production deployment.

KBC's numbers are staggering — 80 million conversations, 350 FTE equivalent output. That is not a pilot. That is a transformation.

For infrastructure architects evaluating their AI platform strategy, the combination of AgentCore (operations) + Managed Agents (frontier models) + S3 (unified data) represents a compelling full-stack offering.
