## Configuration Management Day @ CfgMgmtCamp 2025

Held in Ghent 🇧🇪 as part of the legendary **Configuration Management Camp**, this year’s event spotlighted **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**, **DevOps culture**, and **automation leadership** — with voices from across the open source spectrum.

---

## Featured Presentation: Luca Berton at CfgMgmtCamp 2025

![Luca Berton presenting at CfgMgmtCamp 2025](/blog/conferences/CfgMgmtCamp-2025.jpg)

### **Automating AI-Powered Graph Databases with Ansible: A Neo4j GenAI Case Study**

Luca Berton opened Configuration Management Day 2025 with a cutting-edge session focused on automating Neo4j-based **GenAI environments** using Ansible.

In this practical deep-dive, Luca demonstrated how Ansible can:
- Automate **Neo4j Ops** across cloud and hybrid environments
- Manage GenAI workloads and **infrastructure orchestration** efficiently
- Integrate **event-driven automation** into data pipelines
- Use callback plugins and inventories for performance tuning at scale

### Around the Camp

CfgMgmtCamp fills the lecture halls at HOGENT's Schoonmeersen campus in Ghent. Every room is packed with people who run infrastructure for a living.

![A full lecture hall at CfgMgmtCamp 2025 in Ghent during the Relics of Evolution talk about Chef](/blog/events/cfgmgmtcamp-2025/cfgmgmtcamp-2025-chef-relics-of-evolution.jpg)

*"Relics of Evolution": a look back at how Chef shaped configuration management.*

![A speaker presenting Secrets in Ansible to a classroom audience at CfgMgmtCamp 2025 in Ghent](/blog/events/cfgmgmtcamp-2025/cfgmgmtcamp-2025-secrets-in-ansible.jpg)

*"Secrets in Ansible": the kind of practical session the Ansible track at CfgMgmtCamp is known for.*

AI was on the main-stage programme as well. "An AI LeaderBoard (What did AI ever do for me?)" had models write Ansible, Puppet, Terraform and Bash, then checked whether the output ran.

![The title slide An AI LeaderBoard, What did AI ever do for me, by Karanbir Singh, in front of a full lecture hall at CfgMgmtCamp 2025](/blog/events/cfgmgmtcamp-2025/cfgmgmtcamp-2025-ai-leaderboard.jpg)

*"An AI LeaderBoard (What did AI ever do for me?)" on the big screen in the main hall.*

![A slide titled Does it Run, saying the generated content all deploys but there are gaps, with bar charts for Ansible, Puppet, Terraform and Bash](/blog/events/cfgmgmtcamp-2025/cfgmgmtcamp-2025-ai-leaderboard-does-it-run.jpg)

*"Does it Run?": the generated content all deployed, with gaps, and the Bash column scored lowest.*
