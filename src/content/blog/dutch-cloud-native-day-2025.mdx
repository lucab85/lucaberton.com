## 1. Why this event mattered

After a strong debut in Amsterdam last year, the **Dutch Cloud Native Day (DCND)** returned with a bigger venue, three parallel tracks and a single ambition – _re‑energise the Benelux cloud‑native community_. The 2025 edition delivered on that promise, pairing deep‑dive talks and practical workshops with a hallway track that felt more like a reunion than a conference.

## 2. First impressions – registration & vibe

The day kicked off inside Utrecht’s Jaarbeurs **Supernova** with fast check‑in queues, neon‑striped lights and a _table-full of OSS stickers_. Crew shirts, lanyards and friendly volunteers set the tone: contributor‑first, ego‑last.

## 3. Content highlights

| Time  | Track      | Session | Why it resonated |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------------|
| 10:10 | *Progress* | **Staying Human in a Machine‑Accelerated World** | A timely reminder that empathy scales even when clusters do. |
| 11:10 | *Mission 2* | **The Absolute Beginner’s Guide to Cloud Native** | Standing‑room only – proof the ecosystem still on‑boards newcomers. |
| All‑day | *CFP track* | Lightning talks on eBPF + runtime security | Opinionated & practical, exactly what platform teams need. |

## 4. Hallway track = real value

Between sessions the expo floor became an impromptu ideas market:

* **Book exchanges** – a stack of _[Kubernetes Recipes](https://kubernetes.recipes/)_ turned into the perfect ice‑breaker.

* **Sponsor demos** – Platinum booths (Exoscale, Mirantis, HCS Company) doubled‑down on internal developer platforms. **Isovalent** previewed new Tetragon features – fitting given the eBPF buzz.
* **Career serendipity** – impromptu chats on GPU tenancy, GitOps bootstrapping and SOC‑2 evidence automation translated into concrete follow‑ups.

### Talks I photographed

A few slides from the main room give a more specific picture than the timetable above. The speakers' names are not on these slides, so I leave them out.

![Slide Highly Available PostgreSQL Cluster showing a Read-Write Service and a Read-Only Service across three availability zones, with a primary and synchronous and potentially synchronous standbys on separate worker nodes, and a speaker on stage](/blog/events/dutch-cloud-native-day-2025/postgres-ha-slide.jpg)

*A highly available PostgreSQL cluster on Kubernetes: three availability zones, one primary and two standbys, each on its own worker node with PGDATA and WAL volumes, behind a read-write and a read-only service.*

![Slide Was it worth the effort comparing a ClusteredCron setup with KubeCron across isolation, support, toil, platform and app abstractions, troubleshooting and development environment](/blog/events/dutch-cloud-native-day-2025/worth-the-effort-slide.jpg)

*"Was it worth the effort?" compared a ClusteredCron setup with KubeCron. For KubeCron the slide listed runtime and dependency isolation, support limited to the Kubernetes domain, applications that are infrastructure-agnostic and can be deployed on private or public cloud, and similar dev, integration and prod environments. For ClusteredCron it listed complicated support questions across several domains, apps tightly coupled to the platform and infrastructure, and only production plus a limited dev environment.*

## 5. Emerging themes & strategic takeaways

| Theme                | Signal from DCND                                    | Implication for teams                                   |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| **AI × K8s**         | Live demo on wildfire‑prevention ML models running in‑cluster | GPU scheduling & cost‑aware autoscaling climb the backlog. |
| **eBPF everywhere**  | Full sponsor aisle + packed talks                   | eBPF moves from *nice‑to‑have* to *baseline* SRE skill.  |
| **Platform maturity**| ING case study – 1k Helm releases per day           | Focus shifts from cluster plumbing to DevEx & DORA metrics. |
| **Community first**  | PyLadies workshop & diversity tickets               | Local meetups remain the fastest on‑ramp for talent.     |

## 6. Personal highlights

1. **Live demos that worked.** Tetragon traces lighting up, real‑time.
2. **Tangible next‑steps.** Three separate hallway chats already booked in my calendar.
3. **Joy of print.** The selfie‑wall of engineers holding _[Kubernetes Recipes](https://kubernetes.recipes/)_ says physical books still spark delight.
