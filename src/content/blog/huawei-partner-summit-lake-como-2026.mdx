In April 2026 I was at Lake Como for Huawei's **Benelux & Ireland Partner Summit 2026**. The welcome banner at the entrance said exactly that, and Huawei's own 2026 event calendar (more on that below) listed the summit on 15–17 April in Italy. This post covers the strategy session on the afternoon of 15 April. I've already published three posts from Huawei Summit 2026 as separate deep dives, linked at the end. This is the overview that ties them together.

![A full conference room at the Huawei Benelux & Ireland Partner Summit 2026, with the eKit SME solutions slide on the screen](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/partner-summit-room.jpg)

*A full room for the strategy session.*

## The 2026 Key Strategy keynote

The session was **"2026 Key Strategy"**, presented by **Terry Dong**, Enterprise General Manager. The core of it was what Huawei Enterprise wants to focus on this year, and what it offers partners in return.

![Terry Dong on stage in front of his 2026 Key Strategy title slide, with the audience in the foreground](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/terry-dong-2026-key-strategy.jpg)

*Terry Dong opening the 2026 Key Strategy session.*

The "Huawei Enterprise 2026 Strategy" slide split the plan into three areas:

- **Industry Focus**: four target sectors (Education, Healthcare, MSP/ISP and Government), worked through industry events, account mapping, industry solutions and reference sharing.
- **Partner Focus**: deal registration, demo incentives, capability training and proof-of-concept (POC) support.
- **Marketing Focus**: IT media exposure, joint events and social media posts.

My take: for an integrator, deal registration and POC support matter most on that list. They decide whether a partner can afford to put engineering time into a pilot before the customer signs. Picking education, healthcare, MSP/ISP and government also makes sense. These sectors buy campus networks, Wi-Fi and storage in volume, and they often have small IT teams.

## eKit: "4+10+N" for SMEs

The other slide on screen around the opening of the keynote was **HUAWEI eKit**, billed as **"4+10+N" One-Stop Scenario-Based Solutions for SMEs**. The slide had four scenario tiles:

- **Intelligent Office**: startup workspace and SME office
- **Intelligent Business**: intelligent hotel, store and mall
- **Intelligent Education**: intelligent campus network and interactive classroom
- **Intelligent Healthcare**: digital diagnosis platform, smart consultation room and smart ward

![The HUAWEI eKit 4+10+N One-Stop Scenario-Based Solutions for SMEs slide, with the presenter beside the screen](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/ekit-4-10-n-smes.jpg)

*The eKit slide: four scenarios at the top, the hardware range in the middle, management at the bottom.*

Below the tiles was the hardware range: switches, several access point form factors (settled, high-density, wall plate and outdoor), a Wi-Fi 7 gateway, router, firewall, Mini FTTO gateway and AP, IdeaHub and IdeaHub Mini, storage and SSD. At the bottom was the operations layer: unified selection, deployment and O&M, the **eKit App**, the **eKit.huawei.com** portal, and a cloud management platform covering SME network, OptiX, IdeaHub and storage cloud.

My take: the bundling is the point. SMEs don't want to design a network. They want a known-good kit for "a small office" or "a shop" that a local partner can deploy and manage remotely. The cloud management layer is what lets a partner run that model at a profit.

The keynote then moved on to two product announcements, which I covered in detail at the time:

- **"Pay for Wi-Fi 7, Enjoy the Wi-Fi 8 Experience"**, the pre Wi-Fi 8 AirEngine lineup including the AirEngine 8776I-X6ETHP-T outdoor AP. See [Huawei Wi-Fi 7 AirEngine: Pay for Wi-Fi 7, Get Wi-Fi 8](/blog/huawei-wifi-7-airengine-pre-wifi-8-2026/).

## 2026 goals for the Benelux and Ireland partner business

Before the awards, a slide titled "Together with your support we are confident to achieve our target in 2026" set out the goals for the region. As shown: active partners from 308 to 350, Gold and Silver partners from 13 to 16, partner revenue from 30M to 36M, incentive from 2.3M to 3M and a 30% year-on-year revenue increase. The slide gives no currency or baseline year, so I only quote the numbers as shown.

![Slide reading Together with your support we are confident to achieve our target in 2026, with 2026 goals: revenue year-on-year increase 30%, Gold and Silver partners 13 to 16, active partners 308 to 350, partner revenue 30M to 36M, incentive 2.3M to 3M](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/2026-goals-slide.jpg)

*The 2026 goals slide from the 15 April session.*

## 16 April: the Huawei European Partner Conference

The next day moved to a larger hall at Lake Como. The signage read **"Advancing Together, Integrating Intelligence"**, Huawei European Partner Conference 2026, with the line "Building a Fully Connected, Intelligent World". Names below are only those shown on screen. The Mayor of Como, Alessandro Rapinese, was introduced on a slide, and a slide introduced Leon Zhang, Director of European Enterprise Partner Development & Management at Huawei, before a run of slides titled "Huawei Enterprise Business & Partner Strategy". Later a slide introduced Xia Xingchang, Vice President of European Enterprise Business at Huawei.

Two figures slides set the scene. The first, "Huawei Global: 2025 Business Performance in Line with Forecasts", showed 2025 corporate revenue of CNY880.9 Bn and, among the progress points, 60Mn 5G-A users, more than 7Mn developers and 9,800+ ISV partners for Kunpeng and Ascend, HarmonyOS installations at the 36Mn mark, and 34 Huawei Cloud regions with 101 availability zones. These are Huawei's own marketing figures, so treat them as claims.

![Huawei Global 2025 Business Performance in Line with Forecasts: 2025 corporate revenue CNY880.9 Bn and a Key Business Progress panel](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/huawei-global-2025-performance.jpg)

*The global performance slide: revenue chart on the left, key business progress on the right.*

The second was "Year 2025, Update the progress of Partner business" under the heading Double Digital Growth for European enterprise revenue. It listed 5000+ active partners, eKit business up 100%, partner-led business from 25% to 43%, 98% of delivery and service done by partners, more than 90% of business from partners, rebate of 50M+ (up 20%), 17M marketing investment (up 13%), 8.3k certificates (up 1K) and 200+ "golden seeds". As with the 2026 goals, the slide gives no currency.

![Slide titled Year 2025, Update the progress of Partner business, with active partners, eKit business, partner-led share, rebate, marketing investment and certificates](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/2025-partner-business-progress.jpg)

*The 2025 partner business slide, with the Open and Win-Win captions at the bottom.*

My take: the most useful number for an integrator is the partner-led share (25% to 43%) together with the 98% of delivery and service done by partners. It says where Huawei expects partners to carry the work.

In the late afternoon a retail talk showed "The Connected & AI-native Store", with text on screen saying that Vusion and Huawei are already powering 6,000+ stores together. In the evening, a slide titled "2025 European Top Service Partner" listed award winners from Italy, the Czech Republic, Portugal and France.

![Slide titled The Connected and AI-native Store with high precision and data-driven automation, omnichannel and retail media panels](/blog/events/huawei-partner-summit-lake-como-2026/vusion-ai-native-store.jpg)
