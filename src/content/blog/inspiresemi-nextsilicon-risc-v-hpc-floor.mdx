A quick look around the show floor at [RISC-V Summit Europe 2026](/blog/risc-v-summit-europe-2026-bologna-highlights/) in Bologna — this time at the high-performance end of the ecosystem, where **InspireSemi** and **NextSilicon** were showing what RISC-V can do for [HPC and AI](/blog/risc-v-hpc-supercomputing-european-processor/).

![High-performance RISC-V booths at RISC-V Summit Europe 2026 in Bologna](/blog/events/riscv-summit-2026/riscv-summit-44.jpg)

<YouTube id="rKu5r6-xRXg" title="On the floor: InspireSemi and NextSilicon | RISC-V Summit Europe 2026" />

## InspireSemi — RISC-V Supercomputing Accelerators

**InspireSemi** builds **RISC-V supercomputing accelerators** aimed squarely at HPC and AI workloads — the kind of high-throughput silicon that big simulation and training jobs demand. On the booth was **Doug Norton, Chief Marketing Officer**. Putting supercomputing-class accelerators on an open ISA is one of the clearest signs that RISC-V is moving up the performance ladder, well beyond its embedded roots.

## NextSilicon — High-Performance Compute for HPC and Data Centers

**NextSilicon** focuses on **high-performance compute for HPC and data centers**, represented by **Tom Paulick** from Business Development. Their presence rounds out a picture of RISC-V increasingly showing up where raw throughput and [datacenter-scale deployment](/blog/risc-v-datacenter-servers-sovereign-ai-2026/) matter most.

## A Snapshot of High-Performance RISC-V

Seeing these two booths side by side is a useful snapshot of where the [RISC-V ecosystem](/blog/risc-v-ecosystem-2026-vendors-profiles-momentum/) is heading. The story is no longer just microcontrollers and dev boards — it now stretches into [supercomputing and AI acceleration](/blog/risc-v-ai-accelerators-open-silicon-2026/), the same frontier that Europe's [HPC sovereignty efforts](/blog/risc-v-hpc-supercomputing-european-processor/) are targeting. Open silicon is steadily earning a seat at the high-performance table.

<BlogPromoCard variant="newsletter" />

## The Bottom Line

InspireSemi and NextSilicon are two more data points in a clear trend: RISC-V is being taken seriously for the most demanding HPC and AI workloads. A short walk across one show floor in Bologna captured supercomputing accelerators and data-center compute, both built on the open ISA — the high-performance future of RISC-V on display.
