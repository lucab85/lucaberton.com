On Thursday 13 June 2024 the **Dutch Kubernetes / Cloud Native Meetup** held a "**Happy Birthday, Kubernetes!**" evening at Datadog's Amsterdam office, one week after the project turned ten. The Kubernetes blog's anniversary post, [10 Years of Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2024/06/06/10-years-of-kubernetes/), dates the first commit to 6 June 2014 and notes that the project grew out of lessons learned from Google's Borg system. This post is what I saw and photographed at the meetup.

![A white party hat printed with the Kubernetes logo and the words kubernetes 10 years, held up in front of the meetup screen](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/anniversary-hat.jpg)

*The anniversary paper hat that came with the evening.*

## Hosts and format

The "Meet your hosts" slide showed the Cloud Native Netherlands logo and five organisations: ITQ, Datadog, Microsoft, kubespaces.io and SUSE. Among the names readable on it are Martijn Van Tol (ITQ), Tim Meijer (Datadog) and Alessandro Vozza, listed as a CNCF Ambassador at kubespaces.io. The Datadog neon sign was on the wall behind the screen, and the room was full of people wearing paper party hats.

![The Meet your hosts slide with the Cloud Native Netherlands logo and five host organisations: ITQ, Datadog, Microsoft, kubespaces.io and SUSE](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/meet-your-hosts.jpg)

*The hosts slide, cropped to the screen.*

There was also a cake with a "HBD K8S" icing picture of the Kubernetes logo wearing a party hat.

![A slice of layered sponge cake with a printed icing picture of the Kubernetes logo and the text HBD K8S](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/birthday-cake.jpg)

*The birthday cake: HBD K8S.*

## Looking back at the 10 years

Later in the evening, a talk walked through a year-by-year timeline, introduced with the line "Let's look back at the 10 years". The slides I photographed covered 2015 to 2018:

- **2015: CNCF created.** The slide showed the Cloud Native Computing Foundation logo, with a joke subtitle playing on the foundation's name.
- **2016: Minikube, KOPS, Helm.** The slide asked "Are you feeling the YAML yet?" with a cartoon about how long it took to find a missing space in a `deployment.yaml`.
- **2018: AKS, EKS, Containerd, GitOps.** The meme on this slide was about debugging Kubernetes deployments, and the text underneath mentions vendors.

![A timeline slide reading 2015 CNCF created next to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation logo](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/2015-cncf-created.jpg)

*2015: the CNCF is created.*

![A slide reading 2016 Minikube, KOPS, Helm with the Helm logo and a cartoon about a missing space in deployment.yaml](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/2016-minikube-kops-helm.jpg)

*2016: Minikube, KOPS and Helm, and the YAML jokes that came with them.*

![A slide reading 2018 AKS, EKS, Containerd, Gitops next to a meme about debugging Kubernetes deployments](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/2018-managed-kubernetes.jpg)

*2018: managed Kubernetes on Azure and AWS, containerd and GitOps.*

The conclusion slide set the first year against the tenth with two cartoons, and asked "Are we there yet?" My take: that is a fair summary. Day-to-day operations are far smoother than in the early years, but managing YAML, upgrades and the platform around the cluster is still the hard part. That is why so many teams now talk about platform engineering instead of Kubernetes itself.

![The Conclusion slide, with the question Are we there yet and two cartoons labelled 1st Year of Kubernetes and 10th Year of Kubernetes](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/conclusion-year-1-vs-10.jpg)

*Conclusion: the 1st year of Kubernetes against the 10th.*

## A platform product at RTL

The next talk, from a platform team at **RTL** (TCP on the "Our Team" slide), was titled "The journey to a platform product". Its sections, from the slide, were Shadow IT ("start from within"), Platform Team, Platform as a product, Adoption and Future. The slide named RTL, Videoland, RTL Nieuws and Buienradar.

The team slides defined what a platform team is not. Sysops: "Close, but no, it might overlap". DevOps: "NO! GOD NO! It's not a team. It's not a person! It's a mindset!". GitOps: "A nice tool on the belt but it's not a team". ChatOps: "Hello IT, have you tried turning it off and on again".

![A slide titled Platform team: What it is not, with four boxes for Sysops, Devops, Gitops and Chatops](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/platform-team-what-it-is-not.jpg)

*What a platform team is not.*

The "Adoption at RTL" slide ("How to take over the world?") listed four consumers of the platform. Videoland was described as the main reason and entry point. Buienradar started in Belgium and is slowly moving the Dutch parts over. RTL Nieuws was onboarded on its own duplicate platform and is migrating to the actual one. UDP, the data platform used by RTL data scientists and developers, was described as a daughter team of TCP that uses another duplicate platform.

![A slide titled Adoption at RTL, How to take over the world, with four tiles for Videoland, Buienradar, RTL Nieuws and a data platform](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/adoption-at-rtl.jpg)

*Adoption at RTL: four consumers, at different stages of migration.*

My take: adopting a platform by onboarding the most motivated team first, then migrating the others in stages, is how most internal platforms actually grow. The "platform as a product" framing on the slide is the part that makes it work.

## What is next for the community

The "What's next" slide, shown early in the evening, listed the plans: KCDs (Kubernetes Community Days) "are on hold", the next meetups were a homelab-kube session in July and one in Amersfoort in September, and the TAG Environmental Sustainability week was on 10 October. Another slide on the evening was an announcement for an "Inspiration Event: Real Life Examples for working with Green Software Principles" on 4 July 2024 in Amsterdam. An earlier talk, before the timeline, covered a public transport operator serving 1.200.000 daily travelers and showed its annual service-quality figures.

![Slide "Inspiration Event: Real Life Examples for working with Green Software Principles" with an event card for 4 July 2024 in Amsterdam](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/green-software-inspiration-event-slide.jpg)

*The green software announcement slide.*

The event card on that slide gave the details: 4 July 2024, 17:00 to 19:30 in Amsterdam, hosted through the Green Software - The Netherlands group, with CloudXcellence, the Green Software Foundation and Ortec Finance shown as organisers. I went to that evening too: see [carbon-aware HPC with KEDA and space shifting](/blog/carbon-aware-hpc-keda-space-shifting-meetup-2024/).

![A slide reading What's next: KCDs are on hold; next meetups homelab-kube in July and Amersfoort in Sept; TAG Environmental Sustainability week (October 10th); Your ideas](/blog/events/kubernetes-10th-birthday-amsterdam-2024/whats-next.jpg)

*What's next for the Dutch community.*
