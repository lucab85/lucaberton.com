# MCPA Certification: Validating MCP Expertise at AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe

At **AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe** in Amsterdam, the **Agentic AI Foundation** announced the launch of the **Model Context Protocol Associate (MCPA)** certification — the first vendor-neutral credential validating engineering and platform builders' understanding of the Model Context Protocol.

![RAI Amsterdam expo floor where the MCPA certification was announced during AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026](/blog/events/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-2026/agntcon-2026-07.jpg)

*The RAI Amsterdam expo floor — the MCPA certification was one of several announcements from the Agentic AI Foundation during AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe.*

## What Is MCPA?

The MCPA certification covers:

- **MCP concepts** — the fundamentals of how the protocol connects AI agents to tools, data, and services
- **Architecture** — the design patterns and system-level decisions that make MCP work at scale
- **Implementation considerations** — practical guidance for building, deploying, and operating MCP-compatible systems

The exam is designed for developers, platform engineers, and technical architects who are building or evaluating agentic AI systems using MCP.

## Why MCP Certification Matters Now

MCP has gone from a protocol experiment to the most adopted agent standard in just over a year. With **89% adoption** among agentic AI practitioners (per the 2024 AAIF Member Pulse Survey), MCP has become the de facto standard for agent-to-tool connectivity.

As David Soria Parra, co-creator of MCP and Member of Technical Staff at Anthropic, noted in his keynote at AGNTCon: *"MCP's move to a stateless architecture and formal deprecation policy means enterprises can now adopt it with confidence."*

The MCPA certification gives practitioners a way to validate their knowledge of these evolving standards — from the basics of MCP's stateless core to the new extensions framework and enterprise authorization changes.

## Who Should Take MCPA?

The certification is aimed at:

- **Open source developers** building MCP servers and clients
- **Platform engineers** integrating MCP into enterprise infrastructure
- **Developer relations professionals** supporting MCP adoption
- **Enterprise architects** designing agentic AI systems at scale

## Availability

The MCPA certification exam is available starting today through the Agentic AI Foundation's training portal. Registration and exam details are available at [pr@aaif.io](mailto:pr@aaif.io).
