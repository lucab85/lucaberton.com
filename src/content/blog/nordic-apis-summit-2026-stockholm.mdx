## Topics at Nordic APIs Summit 2026

The 2026 program focuses on the most pressing challenges and innovations shaping modern platform development.

### Next-Generation API-First Strategies

APIs sit at the core of modern enterprise architecture. Sessions will explore how teams design, secure, standardize, and scale API-driven development across products, platforms, and internal engineering organizations.

### Identity and Access Management

As platforms become more interconnected and modular, identity sprawl becomes a real operating risk. Expect discussion around governance, zero-trust security, and access control patterns for increasingly distributed ecosystems.

### Data Sovereignty and Compliance

Regulation and national data priorities are reshaping API strategies. The summit will cover how teams balance openness, interoperability, privacy, and control when data moves across systems and jurisdictions.

### Cloud-Agnostic Architectures

Modern workloads need deployment flexibility across cloud, on-premises, and edge environments. API architecture plays a central role in keeping those systems portable without losing operational consistency.

### Advances in AI

Generative AI and autonomous agents introduce new architectural requirements. APIs become the contracts that expose capabilities, constrain behavior, integrate enterprise systems, and make AI applications governable.

### Industry Case Studies

Leaders from regulated sectors such as finance and healthcare will share real-world approaches to securing and scaling API-driven microservices ecosystems.

## APIs in the Agentic Era

The agentic era changes the API conversation. When software agents can plan, call tools, retrieve context, and act across systems, API design becomes a security and governance discipline as much as an integration discipline.

That raises practical questions:

- Which APIs should agents be allowed to call?
- How should identity and authorization work for delegated actions?
- Where do rate limits, quotas, and approval flows belong?
- How do platform teams expose capabilities without creating unbounded risk?
- How do organizations keep API catalogs, contracts, and governance useful as AI accelerates development?

These are exactly the kinds of questions API and platform teams need to answer before agentic systems become business-critical.
