Every conference recap I write ends up being about people. [Open Source Summit Europe 2026](/blog/open-source-summit-europe-2026-prague-recap/) in Prague was no exception. As a media partner for my interview show [Udienza](https://www.udienza.com/), I had a full calendar of ten-minute recordings. The best moments, though, often came in between: in the corridor, at a booth, or at the reception at Žofín Palace.

Here are the people I met, roughly in the order the week happened. Thank you all for your time.

## The Udienza crew

![Luca Berton and Marcin Czupryniak in Udienza t-shirts on the first morning](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-udienza-crew-morning.jpg)

None of the interviews would exist without **Marcin Czupryniak**, who filmed and recorded audio for Udienza all week. We started Wednesday morning in matching black t-shirts and kept going until the last hour before my flight on Friday.

![Luca Berton in front of the Open Source Summit Europe 2026 sponsor wall](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-sponsor-wall.jpg)
*Udienza was listed as a media partner on the event website. That is worth a photo in front of the sponsor wall.*

## Wednesday: kernel people and the first booths

**Greg Kroah-Hartman**, the Linux stable kernel maintainer, met me by the badge desk on Wednesday morning. He also wrote the foreword of the State of Open Source in Europe report launched that day. More in [Kernel Maintainers in Prague](/blog/oss-europe-2026-kernel-maintainers-greg-kh-arnaldo-melo/).

![Luca Berton with Greg Kroah-Hartman](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-greg-kroah-hartman.jpg)

**Carol Chen**, a speaker at the summit, stopped for a photo with Marcin and me just before the press panel started.

![Luca Berton, Marcin Czupryniak and Carol Chen](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-carol-chen.jpg)

**Arnaldo Carvalho de Melo** of Red Hat, maintainer of pahole and perf tooling, recorded with us in the afternoon. He was still wearing the lavalier mic when we took this photo.

![Luca Berton with Arnaldo Melo and Marcin Czupryniak](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-arnaldo-melo-interview.jpg)

**Kavitha Daula**, VP of Engineering at **Edera**, hosted us at their booth for a conversation on hypervisor isolation for containers and AI agents. See [Isolating AI Agents](/blog/oss-europe-2026-isolating-ai-agents-edera-coder-openbao/).

At the end of the day I found the **OpenBao** crew near the OpenSSF booth: **Toni Tauro** (Adfinis) and **Andrii Fedorchuk**, with Marcin in the middle as usual.

![Luca Berton, Marcin Czupryniak, Andrii Fedorchuk and Toni Tauro](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-openbao-toni-tauro.jpg)

I also met **benny Vasquez**, chair of the AlmaLinux OS Foundation board, on the floor on Wednesday. We set up a proper conversation for the next day.

The evening belonged to **Žofín Palace** and the 35 years of Linux casino reception. I wrote that up separately in [35 Years of Linux at Žofín Palace](/blog/oss-europe-2026-35-years-of-linux-zofin-palace/).

## Thursday: foundations and the show floor

Thursday started with **Ben Potter**, Head of Developer Relations at **Coder**, for a recording on cloud development environments and AI coding agents. Thanks to Cassandra Jowett at Coder for setting it up.

In the afternoon I ran into **Ralph Squillace** and **Marco D'Angelo** (Radixia) in one of the lounges.

![Luca Berton with Ralph Squillace, Marco D'Angelo and a colleague](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-ralph-squillace-marco-dangelo.jpg)

Then came the CNCF session with **Bob Killen** and **Daniel Krook**, arranged by Haley White and the Linux Foundation PR team.

![Luca Berton with the CNCF team](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-cncf-interview-room.jpg)

After that I visited the **AlmaLinux** booth for my conversation with benny Vasquez on community-governed enterprise Linux. See [Neutral Governance](/blog/oss-europe-2026-foundations-governance-cncf-opensearch-almalinux/).

Near the OpenSSF area I met **Alex Scheel** of Control Plane with Toni again. Control Plane is one of the contributing vendors behind the OSERA initiative I wrote about in [AI-Speed Exploits](/blog/oss-europe-2026-ai-speed-exploits-open-secure-ai-alliance-osera/).

![Luca Berton with Alex Scheel and Toni Tauro](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-openbao-alex-scheel.jpg)

A few minutes later, on the same floor, I met a group that included **Petr Hodina**, a speaker, and **Michelle Willemse** of Adfinis.

![Luca Berton with Petr Hodina, Michelle Willemse and other attendees](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-adfinis-group.jpg)

The day ended at the **Igalia** booth with **Mario Sánchez Prada**, who had just given his WPE WebKit session. More in [Embedded Linux in Prague](/blog/oss-europe-2026-embedded-linux-ti-igalia-risc-v/).

## Friday: Linus, Flatcar and the last hallway

Friday started with the Linus Torvalds and Dirk Hohndel keynote. After that came my slot with **Thilo Fromm** of Flatcar Container Linux at the Microsoft booth, and a planned catch-up with **Paula Grzegorzewska** of Linux Foundation Europe, who had reached out to Udienza herself during the week.

Before lunch I bumped into **Diana Todea** of VictoriaMetrics and a fellow attendee in the main hall.

![Luca Berton with Diana Todea and another attendee](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-diana-todea.jpg)

Then a last stop at the **openEuler** booth before heading to the airport.

![Luca Berton with the openEuler team at their booth](/blog/events/open-source-summit-2026/oss-eu-2026-openeuler-booth.jpg)

## New connections after the event

The LinkedIn requests that followed tell the story of the week. They included **Nithya Ruff**, Chair of the Linux Foundation Board; **Johan Linåker** of RISE, her co-presenter in the [Open Source at a Crossroads keynote](/blog/oss-europe-2026-open-source-crossroads-sovereignty/); **Juliette van der Laarse**, founding director of the AI Flower Foundation, whose Friday keynote argued for moving "from a thousand private agents to one open garden"; Brian "bex" Exelbierd; Andrew Randall of Azure; and engineers from Red Hat, SAP, Ericsson and JetBrains.

## What I learned about the hallway track

1. **Book the interviews, but leave gaps.** Half of the people above were not on my calendar.
2. **Wear something recognisable.** The Udienza t-shirt started more conversations than any business card.
3. **Follow up within 24 hours.** By Friday afternoon, the people I had messaged on Wednesday evening were the ones I had real second conversations with.

If we met in Prague and you are not on this list, it is my memory, not you. Send me a message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucaberton/). For the format of these roundups, see also [People I Met at AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026](/blog/agntcon-people-met-2026/).
