The program at **Summit: Connect 2026 Utrecht** ran on two parallel theater tracks, and both were dense with practical, production-grade AI and platform content. Here is the full rundown, taken from the printed agenda boards on the floor.

![Theater 1's breakout agenda at Red Hat Summit: Connect 2026 Utrecht.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-agenda/rhsc-nl-2026-agenda-theater1.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }

## Theater 1 — the Dell & ITQ "AI Use Case Factory"

Theater 1 was effectively a **Dell & ITQ** showcase, built around a recurring theme: turning AI ideas into running production systems. The series ran as a set of short, concrete case studies.

| Time | Title | Speaker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:50–09:05 | **From Idea to Production: Introducing the ITQ AI Use Case Factory** | Maxime Colomès, AI Engineer, ITQ |
| 11:00–11:15 | **From On-Prem to Cloud: Migration and Modernisation with Red Hat and Azure** | Joran Bergfeld, Microsoft |
| 11:30–11:45 | **No Factory Required: Building MP Motorsport's Virtual Race Engineer** | Tom Heyerick, AI Engineer, ITQ |
| 12:00–12:15 | **Beyond the Stopwatch: Building an AI Human Performance Engine for Professional Athletes** | Christiaan Roelofield, Cloud-Native Consultant, ITQ |
| 12:30–12:45 | **GPUs Take Flight: Multi-Tenant Platform Engineering at Scale** | Luca Berton, Senior Consultant, Dell Technologies |
| 13:00–13:15 | **From Idea to Production: Introducing the ITQ AI Use Case Factory** | Maxime Colomès, AI Engineer, ITQ |

A few of these deserve a closer look:

- **MP Motorsport's Virtual Race Engineer** — a domain-specific AI that reasons about a racing car in real time, the kind of low-latency, high-stakes workload that pushes you straight into multi-tenant GPU scheduling.
- **An AI Human Performance Engine for professional athletes** — moving an AI system out of the lab and into a live sports environment, where reliability and timing matter as much as accuracy.
- **Migration and Modernisation with Red Hat and Azure** — the on-prem-to-cloud path that so many enterprises are navigating right now, done the Red Hat way.

And then, at 12:30, my own session: **GPUs Take Flight — Multi-Tenant Platform Engineering at Scale**, the talk that ties the whole morning together. If you are running AI workloads and need a platform that can schedule, isolate and share GPUs across tenants without the chaos, this is the one.

> **Read:** [GPUs Take Flight: Multi-Tenant Platform Engineering at Red Hat Summit: Connect Utrecht 2026](/blog/gpus-take-flight-red-hat-summit-connect-utrecht-2026/)

## Theater 2 — security, automation and delivery

Theater 2 leaned into the other half of the stack: security, infrastructure automation and the path from code to customer.

![Theater 2's breakout agenda at Red Hat Summit: Connect 2026 Utrecht.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-agenda/rhsc-nl-2026-agenda-theater2.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }

| Time | Title |
| --- | --- |
| 08:50–09:05 | **SoftwareOne & IBM — What do I build on?** |
| 11:00–11:15 | **Devoceam — From CVE to Production Fix: A Real-World Case Study** |
| 11:30–11:45 | **Dell & ITQ — Private AI: From Concept to Production** |
| 12:00–12:15 | **Dell & ITQ — Infrastructure Automation and OpenShift at Scale with Terraform and Ansible** |
| 12:30–12:45 | **Illionx / PostNL — AI-Native Delivery: From Code to Customer** |
| 13:00–13:15 | **SoftwareOne & IBM — Project Lightwell & Rogue AI** |

The standout for the security-minded was the closing **Project Lightwell & Rogue AI** slot — a look at how a real-world CVE travels from discovery to a production fix, with the uncomfortable truth that zero-day exploitation pressure is up roughly **52% year on year**. It pairs directly with the Lightwell deep-dive.

> **Read:** [Red Hat Lightwell: From CVE to Production Fix at Summit Connect 2026 Utrecht](/blog/red-hat-lightwell-cve-to-production-fix-summit-connect-2026/)

## The throughline

Read the two boards side by side and a clear story emerges. **Theater 1 is about building** — factories, engines, virtual race engineers, multi-tenant GPU platforms. **Theater 2 is about securing, automating and delivering** it — from a CVE to a fix, from Terraform and Ansible to a shipped product, from code to customer. That is the whole production-AI loop in one day: prototype, platform, production, proof.
