Rain, a cold November wind off the river, and a packed house at NBC Nieuwegein. That was the scene on the morning of 11 November 2026, when Red Hat opened **Summit: Connect** in Utrecht — the EMEA regional edition of Red Hat Summit — with a full program of AI, OpenShift and Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security breakouts spread across two theaters.

Here is how the morning unfolded.

## Arrival

The venue was busy by 08:30. Attendees streamed in through the NBC Nieuwegein foyer to the opening keynote, and the first images of the day are the ones that matter most: people arriving, lanyards on, eyes on the screens.

![Arrival at NBC Nieuwegein for Red Hat Summit: Connect 2026 Utrecht.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-morning/rhsc-nl-2026-morning-arrival.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }

## The sponsor wall

A good way to read a community event is to read its sponsors. The wall at Summit Connect Utrecht announced **Dell | ITQ** as the Diamond sponsor, flanked by Kangaroot and the HCS company (Tech Tribes), with Intel, Dell and IBM across the top.

![The sponsor wall at Red Hat Summit: Connect 2026 Utrecht — Dell | ITQ as Diamond sponsor.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-morning/rhsc-nl-2026-morning-sponsors.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }

It tells you where the momentum in the ecosystem sits: hardware and platform partners (Dell, Intel), a services house (ITQ, Dell's own AI and cloud-native partner), and the community layer (Kangaroot, Tech Tribes) that keeps OpenShift and AI useful in the real world.

## Before the keynote

By the time **Andrew Brown** took the stage, the main theater was full. The countdown on the screen hit zero and the room went quiet.

![The crowd in the main theater before Andrew Brown's opening keynote.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-morning/rhsc-nl-2026-morning-crowd.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }

If you missed it, the headline numbers from Brown's opening are worth a full post on their own: **90%** of organizations have adopted AI but only **24%** of the value has actually landed; **98%** of developers are already using AI in their work; and just **40%** of that AI is running in production. The throughline: the ground has shifted, and the gap between "we use AI" and "AI is running in production" is where the real work lives.

## The morning breakouts

With the keynote done, the morning moved into the two theater tracks. Theater 1 opened its doors to the Dell & ITQ "AI Use Case Factory" series and a Microsoft session on migrating on-prem workloads to Azure with Red Hat. Theater 2 ran its own parallel slate. Both agendas were printed and on the wall.

![Theater 1's morning breakout agenda at Summit Connect Utrecht.](/blog/events/red-hat-summit-connect-nl-2026-morning/rhsc-nl-2026-morning-theater1.jpg){ loading="lazy" decoding="async" }
