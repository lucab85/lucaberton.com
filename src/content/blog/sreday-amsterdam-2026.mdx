On Monday 2 November I was at **SREday Amsterdam 2026**, in ING Cedar on the Bijlmerdreef: one day, from 08:00 to 18:30, four tracks in four event halls. I came as a speaker, with a talk on the main stage in the afternoon, and with my microphones for [Udienza](/blog/udienza-video-podcast-launch-2026/), my video podcast. This is the day as I saw it, from the programme, the slides and the five conversations I recorded.

## The keynotes: who watches the agents?

The main stage opened with four keynotes. Peter Marshall of Imply opened with *Agentic observability needs a new engine*. Lewis Isaac of Coralogix asked it outright: *Who's Watching the Agents? Measuring the Impact of AI-Assisted Development*. Stephen Cihak of Harness went one step further, to the agent that fixes what it finds: *Closing the Loop: From Production Incident to Autonomous Fix*. And Miko Pawlikowski, who co-founded SREday, brought *Ten years of Kubernetes lessons, in one binary*.

![Peter Marshall on the main stage for the first keynote, an Imply slide behind](/blog/events/sreday-amsterdam-2026/sreday-2026-imply-keynote.jpg)

## A GPU, curled into the cluster

After lunch I sat in event hall 2. Thijs Feryn of Varnish Software went first, on what is new in Varnish, from TLS handled natively to a gateway that does HTTP routing for Kubernetes.

Piotr Zaniewski of vCluster Labs followed, and his title meant exactly what it said: he curled a GPU into his cluster. On stage, the control plane and one worker lived on his laptop; the GPU was a Google Cloud VM with an NVIDIA T4, which got its driver, Ollama and a model at first boot, then joined the laptop's cluster through a tunnel. The private-nodes slide listed what any host needs to join, whether a KVM VM, a cloud VM or bare metal: one curl command, an expiring join token, and a VPN built on the Tailscale SDK and WireGuard, with no Tailscale account.

![Event hall 2 before Piotr Zaniewski's talk, vCluster on the screen](/blog/events/sreday-amsterdam-2026/sreday-2026-vcluster.jpg)

At 16:30 it was my turn, on the main stage, with *The GPU Is Not Your SLO: Reliability Engineering for Production AI*.

## The conversations

I recorded five conversations for [Udienza](https://www.udienza.com/) during the day, each an episode with its own page:

- [Peter Marshall](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/peter-marshall), Imply: logs, compression, and agents let loose on an investigation.
- [Mark Pawlikowski](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/mark-pawlikowski), SREday: the conference kept small on purpose, LLMday and PLATFORMday.
- [Piotr Zaniewski](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/piotr-zaniewski), vCluster Labs: GPUs on demand for developers, and the free tiers.
- [Stephen Cihak](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/stephen-cihak), Harness: agents that deploy code, safely.
- [Lewis Isaac](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/lewis-isaac), Coralogix: watching the tokens and the cost of code agents.
