## What a media partnership is, and is not

Two partnerships, each for one event and each confirmed in writing by the organiser: AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026 and Open Source Summit Europe 2026. At the KubeCons the show held media accreditation, which is a press badge, not a partnership. A partnership opens the rooms: the schedule early, the press room, introductions, a logo and a mention. It gives nobody a say in who sits in the other chair or what gets asked. The longer version, and the reasoning, is in the [article on udienza.com](https://www.udienza.com/articles/where-udienza-films-2026).
