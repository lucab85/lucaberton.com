Udienza films where the people who build what you run already are: the booth, the hallway, the speaker room. Not a studio. In 2026 that has meant five conferences in four cities, and two of them list the show as a media partner. This is where the episodes come from, what the show did in each room, and what a media partnership is and is not.

## FOSDEM 2026, Brussels

31 January and 1 February, at the ULB campus. More than 8,000 people, 1,013 talks, and the hallway track that FOSDEM is known for.

The show's first conversation was recorded there: [Monty Widenius, creator of MySQL and MariaDB](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/monty-widenius), episode 01. Where is MariaDB going in 2026, and does it still speak MySQL? It first appeared here as a [written interview](/blog/fosdem-2026-monty-widenius-mariadb-interview/); the episode page adds the chapters, the takeaways and the full transcript. The weekend itself is in [FOSDEM 2026](/blog/fosdem-2026/) and the [hallway interviews](/blog/fosdem-2026-hallway-interviews-brussels/).

No partnership, no badge beyond a visitor's. FOSDEM has no press programme, and the show needed none: the people were in the corridors.

## KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2026, Amsterdam

23 to 26 March, RAI Amsterdam. The host held media accreditation from the CNCF and also spoke, on [orchestrating multi-tenant GPUs on OpenShift AI](/blog/kubecon-2026-talk-multi-tenant-gpus-recap/).

Three episodes come from that week, all filmed in the interview rooms and on the floor:

- [Kat Cosgrove, Minimus and the Kubernetes Steering Committee](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/kat-cosgrove), episode 02. Is the open source in your cluster part of your security posture? ([written interview](/blog/kat-cosgrove-kubecon-eu-2026-security-minimus/))
- [Sven Mößbauer, Dash0](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/sven-dash0), episode 03. Can observability run on open standards without the lock-in? ([written interview](/blog/dash0-kubecon-eu-2026-observability-opentelemetry/))
- [Luca Galante, Platform Engineering](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/luca-galante), episode 04. Does AI make platform teams more necessary, or less? ([written interview](/blog/luca-galante-kubecon-eu-2026-platform-engineering-ai/))

A fourth conversation from Amsterdam, with [Guido Laout of OVHcloud](/blog/guido-laout-ovhcloud-kubecon-europe-2026-amsterdam-sovereignty/) on what a European public cloud is for, is cut and waiting its Tuesday as episode 10. The week in pictures is in the [KubeCon Europe 2026 recap](/blog/kubecon-europe-2026-recap-week-in-photos/).

## KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan 2026, Yokohama

28 to 30 July. Media accreditation again, which meant the [CNCF press briefing](/blog/kubecon-japan-2026-cncf-japan-briefing/) on 29 July and a seat for the [AI platform keynotes](/blog/kubecon-japan-2026-ai-platform-keynotes/).

One episode: [Chris Aniszczyk, CTO of the CNCF](https://www.udienza.com/episodes/chris-aniszczyk), episode 05. Is your Kubernetes platform ready for agents? Agents as the new microservices, what ten more years of AI do to infrastructure, and how a young engineer should start. Chris had already spoken with the host in Amsterdam in April; that [written conversation](/blog/chris-aniszczyk-cncf-cto-kubecon-europe-2026-agentic-ai-kubernetes/) is the long version. What the community took home is in the [Japan takeaways](/blog/kubecon-japan-2026-community-takeaways/).

## AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026, Amsterdam

17 and 18 September, RAI Amsterdam again: more than 2,000 people at the Agentic AI Foundation's first European event, produced with the Linux Foundation. This was the show's first media partnership, confirmed by the Linux Foundation's events team in August and listed under the host's name, because the show did not have its name yet.

Five conversations were recorded on the floor, all on the same question from different seats: what has to be true for an agent to run in production.

- Manik Surtani, CTO and co-founder of the AAIF, on agents, MCP and production. Episode 06.
- Frédéric Barthelet, CTO and co-founder of Alpic and author of Skybridge, who gave the keynote comparing MCP, WebMCP and the CLI, on running MCP in production. Episode 07.
- Theo of Valency, on agent infrastructure and the parts of MCP that only show up at scale. Episode 08.
- Shannon Williams of Obot AI, on agents inside the enterprise and what a company has to change to trust one. Episode 09.
- Dilip Lukose, co-founder and CPO of Orkes, on orchestrating agentic systems. Episode 11.

The two days are written up in [day one](/blog/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-2026/), [day two](/blog/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-day2-2026/), the [key takeaways](/blog/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-2026-recap-takeaways/) and the [people met](/blog/agntcon-people-met-2026/); the [preview](/blog/agntcon-mcpcon-europe-2026-media-partner/) is where the partnership was announced. The recap above is cut from that week.

Last year's edition, in Amsterdam, is in the [Open Source Summit Europe 2025 recap](/blog/open-source-summit-europe-2025-amsterdam-recap/).

## What a media partnership is, and is not

Udienza has two media partnerships, each for one event and each confirmed in writing by the organiser: AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026 and Open Source Summit Europe 2026. At KubeCon Europe and KubeCon Japan the show held media accreditation, which is a press badge, not a partnership. The footer of [udienza.com](https://www.udienza.com/) says which is which, and nothing goes there without the organiser's written confirmation.

A partnership gives the show the schedule early, the press room, introductions to speakers, a logo on the event page and a mention from the organiser. It does not give anyone a say in who sits in the other chair or what gets asked. Guests are chosen for what they built. Sponsors never pick the guest, the questions or the edit, and neither do partners.

For a ten-minute show, that access is the format. The people in these episodes do not have an hour and would not fly to a studio; they are at these events anyway, and the show meets them there.
