On 23 April 2026 I went to a short evening talk in Amsterdam titled **"A Real Founder Story: Manage your Ego, before it Manages your Company"**. The title slide named the speaker as **Anthony Meijer zu Schlochtern, Serial Entrepreneur and Founder of ZEAL Partners**. The slides did not show an organiser, so I will not guess who hosted. This is a non-technical post, but founders in infrastructure and AI companies run into exactly the patterns he described.

![Title slide: A Real Founder Story, Manage your Ego, before it Manages your Company, Anthony Meijer zu Schlochtern, Serial Entrepreneur and Founder ZEAL Partners](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/title.jpg)
*The title slide. The talk opened with the speaker's own story as a founder.*

## A founder who recognises the failure modes

The story slide, headed "And I...", listed behaviours the speaker presented as his own as a founder:

- was bossing around everyone
- was overpromising to clients
- was overworking and expected everyone to do the same
- was very on edge
- was checking the bank account hourly

The list is useful because it is concrete. These are behaviours, not personality types, and anyone running a company will recognise at least two of them.

## The model: Brains, Power, Social effectiveness

The framework was drawn as a house. The roof is **B for Brains**, and the two rooms below it are **P for Power** and **S for Social effectiveness**.

![House diagram with B Brains on top, P Power and S Social effectiveness below](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/brains-power-social-house.jpg)
*The Brains, Power, Social effectiveness house.*

Two slides then listed typical behaviours for each side, positive and negative. For **S (Social effectiveness)** the positives were: I am vulnerable, I offer support, I am patient, I am thinking in options. The negatives were: I please others, I am postponing, I don't ask my price, I am judgemental.

![Social effectiveness slide listing positive and negative behaviours of a typical S](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/social-behaviours.jpg)
*Typical S behaviours, positive and negative.*

For **P (Power)** the positives were: I show courage, I am decisive, I am applying focus, I am modest. The negatives were: I take too much on my plate, I am in a hurry, I am micromanaging, I am showing off.

![Power slide listing positive and negative behaviours of a typical P](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/power-behaviours.jpg)
*Typical P behaviours: the same drive that makes a founder decisive can turn into micromanaging.*

The point of the two lists is that each strength has a shadow. A power-type founder's decisiveness and a social-type founder's patience each tip into the negative column under stress.

## The positive and negative version of the house

A second version of the house split every part into a plus and a minus side, divided by a band called **"The wall of doubt and shame"**:

- **B+ Wisdom** at the top, **B- Stubbornness** (spelled "Stubbornes" on the slide) at the bottom
- **P+ Experience of abundance** against **P- Experience of scarcity**
- **S+ Experience of unity** against **S- Experience of rejection**

![Positive and negative house: wisdom, experience of abundance, experience of unity, the wall of doubt and shame, experience of scarcity, experience of rejection, stubbornness](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/abundance-scarcity-wall.jpg)
*The wall of doubt and shame separates the plus side from the minus side.*

## From the house to a company

![House diagram relabelled as flight deck, restaurant, kitchen and basement](/blog/events/zeal-partners-founder-story-manage-your-ego-amsterdam-2026/flight-deck-restaurant-kitchen.jpg)
*The same house as a company metaphor: flight deck, restaurant, kitchen, basement.*

The last part mapped the house onto a business: **B+ is the flight deck**, **P+ the restaurant**, **S+ the kitchen**, and the **basement** is the foundation. A following slide listed Promise, External Fire Power, Trial and Money on the left and a second column on the right (partly hidden in my photo, with "Internal Safety Net" legible), connected by a two-way arrow. My reading, not the speaker's words, is that it is about the balance between what a founder promises outside and what the company can back up inside.

The final slides promoted **ZEAL Academy**, with a slide reading "Start New Cohort September 2026" (the Dutch headline read "Pak je volledige potentieel"). The speaker and Ole van der Straaten are listed as co-authors of the Dutch book *ZEAL: Groeitaal voor ondernemers*, published on 23 April 2025 by Santasado, on [Managementboek](https://www.managementboek.nl/boek/9789083490885/zeal-groeitaal-voor-ondernemers-ole-van-der-straaten).

## My take

The checking-the-bank-account-hourly slide was the honest one. In my consulting work, the same shadows show up as over-promising on delivery dates and taking on too much of the architecture work personally. A model like this is not science, but a shared vocabulary for "this is my power-side stress pattern" makes it easier to talk about with a team.
