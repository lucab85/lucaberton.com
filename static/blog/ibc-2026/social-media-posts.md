Three infrastructure trends dominated IBC 2026:

1. **MoQ over Quic** is moving from theory to deployment. 30+ stands demoed the protocol at IBC 2026 (up from 10 in 2025). First use cases will be primary distribution by 2027.

2. **Hybrid cloud is real** — not the multi-cloud decks, but the actual "on-prem for latency, cloud for burst" reality. BCE's sovereign cloud, Netflix's hybrid workflows, BBright's single-stream multilingual delivery.
